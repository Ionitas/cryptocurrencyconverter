## App Description

### Main Description

**CurrencyX** is the ultimate currency converter for modern investors and travelers. Convert between cryptocurrencies, fiat currencies, and precious metals with live exchange rates — all in one beautifully designed app.

Whether you're trading Bitcoin, traveling abroad, or tracking your portfolio, CurrencyX gives you accurate conversions at your fingertips.

### Key Features

#### ⚡ Real-time Exchange Rates
- Live rates updated constantly from trusted sources
- Multiple API fallbacks ensure 99.9% uptime
- Rates cached locally for instant access

#### 🌍 150+ Currencies Supported
- **Cryptocurrencies**: Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, and 100+ more
- **Fiat Currencies**: USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, and 60+ world currencies
- **Precious Metals**: Gold, Silver, Platinum

#### 🧮 Smart Built-in Calculator
- Quick math without leaving the app
- Supports addition, subtraction, multiplication, division
- Clean, intuitive calculator interface

#### 📱 Works Offline
- Cached rates available without internet
- 12-hour cache validity for reliable offline use
- Seamless sync when back online

#### 📊 Portfolio Tracking (Premium)
- Track your crypto & fiat holdings
- Real-time portfolio valuation
- Beautiful charts and analytics

#### 🎨 Beautiful Design
- Modern, minimalist interface
- Three stunning themes: Blue, Dark, Light
- Smooth animations throughout
- Designed for iOS & Android

#### 🔒 Privacy First
- No account required
- No personal data collected
- All data stored locally on device
